💡 Strategy

Things to Know
Before You Go

Pro tips straight from veterans of last year's San Antonio event

1
Arrive at 8AM — not 9AM
Doors open at 8AM for check-in. The expo starts at 9AM sharp. Get there early to avoid the registration line and get your wristband first. The Fenty, Rare Beauty, and Cécred booths will have 40+ minute lines by 9:15.
2
Split up with your friends
If you're going with a group, divide and conquer. Cover different sections simultaneously, then text each other when you find hidden gem booths or freebies. You'll cover 2x the ground.
3
Hit high-ROI booths first
Celebrity brand booths — Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty — draw the longest lines early. Hit those in the first 90 minutes, then loop back to mid-tier booths after noon when crowds thin.
4
Bring MULTIPLE bags
Last year attendees brought full suitcases. No wheels allowed (except the $50 Ulta roller), so pack multiple large tote bags. Polite Society was giving away huge bags — grab those and re-use them throughout the day.
5
Use the shipping station
If you're flying home, use the on-site shipping station to box up your haul before you leave. Boxes and labels are available on-site. Domestic only — no international shipping.
6
Use our Expo Planner tool
Download our free interactive planner to build your custom route, track your checklist, and log visited booths in real time from your phone. Zero wasted time, maximum haul.

Strategy

Entry Day Game Plan

8A
Arrive at 8:00 AM

Check-in line opens. Get wristband and swag bag claim ticket. Use this time to orientate.

9A
Doors open — go RIGHT toward Zone C/D

Most people go left first. Beat the crowd to Fenty (2009), Rare Beauty (2003), Cécred (1903) and Pat McGrath (1821).

11A
Work Zone B (1000–1600s)

PATTERN (1421), COSRX (2240), Tatcha (1803), tarte (1703), Charlotte Tilbury (201), NARS (1403).

1P
Lunch break + Zone A sweep

Grab food, drop bags at storage, then sweep Zone A (100–999s) — CeraVe, Charlotte Tilbury, Lancôme, NYX, Kylie.

4P
Pick up swag bag & ship haul

Collect official swag bag. Use shipping station before 5PM to box up purchases. Storage closes 5:30PM.
