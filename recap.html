2025 highlights

What Went Right

Cécred debut — Beyoncé's booth had the longest line of the entire event (~3 hours peak) and generated the most social content of any booth in UBW history.
K-Beauty wing — COSRX, Tatcha, and TIR TIR grouped in Zone D created a de facto K-beauty destination that attendees mapped out specifically.
Swag bag quality — Multiple attendees received full-size Drunk Elephant, OLAPLEX, and Rare Beauty items. Estimated $1,400–$1,800 total value.
Charlotte Tilbury masterclass — Free 20-min glam sessions were sold out by 9:15AM. Booking early was the move.
What we learned

What to Do Differently

Arrive before 8AM — In 2025, people who arrived at 9AM (expo open) faced 45-min lines immediately at high-demand booths. 8AM check-in gave you a 60-min head start.
Bring rolling luggage if you can — Many attendees ran out of bag space by 1PM. A rolling carry-on would have doubled carry capacity.
Dyson lines were 2+ hours — Most people missed demos entirely. Book a demo appointment via the Dyson app before the event if available in 2026.
Food ran out by 1PM — Food vendors were overwhelmed. Pack your own lunch or eat before you arrive.

How the Day Unfolded at UBW 2025

6:30 AM
Lines form outside OCCC
Early birds started arriving at 6:30AM — 90 minutes before check-in. By 7AM there was a visible queue. Nobody expected this.
~800 people in line by 7AM
8:00 AM
Check-in opens · Immediate Chaos
Check-in was overwhelmed in the first 15 minutes. Some attendees reported 20-min waits just to scan in. Have your ID and ticket on the first screen — don't search for it at the desk.
24,000 check-ins processed in 60 min
9:00 AM
🎉 Expo floor opens — the sprint begins
The floor opened and within 8 minutes the Cécred booth had a queue wrapping to the next zone. First movers who went right to Zone C/D first cleaned up. Zone A (budget brands) was empty until 11AM.
Cécred line: 3hr peak · Fenty: 2hr · Charlotte Tilbury: 90min
11:30 AM
Celebrity sighting peak window
Multiple brand founders appeared 11AM–1PM. Tracee Ellis Ross did a surprise appearance at PATTERN (1421) at 11:45AM, triggering a flood of attendees to Zone B.
12:00 PM
⚠️ Food vendors run dry · Crowd thins at celeb booths
This was the window — celebrity booth lines dropped by 50% as crowds went for lunch. Smart attendees used 12–2PM to hit everything they missed in the morning.
3:00 PM
Afternoon sweep — underrated window
Zone A booths were nearly empty by 3PM. Attendees who saved Zone A for the afternoon had incredible access — no lines, generous samples, personal stylist time.
Best time for Naturium, CeraVe, Kiehl's, Sol de Janeiro
4:00 PM
🎁 Swag bag pickup — don't miss this!
In 2025, some attendees nearly missed the swag bag pickup because they didn't set a reminder. The pickup area had its own 30-min queue. Collect before 4:30PM.
$1,500 average value · Do not leave without it
