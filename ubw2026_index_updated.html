Must Know Before You Go
🆔
ID Required
Physical government-issued ID required. Digital/scanned copies NOT accepted. Name must match ticket exactly. Must be 18+.
🔐
Express Check-In
Your Express Check-In Confirmation will be sent via separate email. Have it ready at registration in West Building D Lobby.
🔒
Security Screening
All attendees must pass through metal detectors. No firearms or weapons permitted. This is a private event.
💳
Cashless Event
OCCC is fully cashless. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay all accepted.
🛄
Roller Bag Pickup
If you purchased a roller bag, pick it up at W222AB after registration. Available day-of only — limit one per registrant.
👜
Bag Policy
Ulta Beauty World wheeled bags are the ONLY wheeled bags allowed. Bring multiple tote bags for everything else!
🧳
Free Luggage Storage
Complimentary luggage storage available on-site. Keep valuables with you — UB is not responsible for lost items.
🎁
Swag Bag ($2,000+)
Pick up from West Hall F3 from 11AM–5:30PM. Must be present in person. One bag per registrant, non-transferable.
📮
FedEx Shipping
On-site FedEx shipping station at West Hall F2 — 11AM–5:30PM. Standard box $35, up to 25 lbs. Domestic only.
📒
UBW Passport
Get stamped at every booth you visit! Pick up your passport at check-in. One per registrant, no replacements.
🥤
Food & Beverage
Concessions available for purchase inside the expo. Outside food not permitted. OCCC is cashless.
👟
Wear Comfy Shoes
You'll walk 5–8 miles on the expo floor. Cute AND comfortable — your feet will thank you later.

Survival Tips
01
Arrive at 8AM — not 9AM
Doors open 8AM for check-in. Fenty, Cécred, and Rare Beauty booths have 40+ min lines by 9:15AM.
02
Split up with your crew
Divide and conquer. Cover different zones simultaneously — text each other about hidden gem booths and freebies.
03
Hit high-ROI booths first
Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty draw the longest lines. Hit those in the first 90 minutes.
04
Bring multiple tote bags
No wheeled bags except the $50 Ulta roller. Some booths give away bags you can reuse all day.
05
Use the on-site shipping station
Flying home? Box up your haul on-site. Use our Shipping Calculator to plan costs ahead.
06
Use the EBD Expo Planner
Build your route, track your checklist, run the Girl Math budget analyzer — all in our free Planner.
