My story

About Jem

I'm a beauty obsessive who went to the inaugural Ulta Beauty World in San Antonio last year and learned a lot from the experience. I watched people leave with half as much as they could have gotten simply because they didn't have a plan.

So for Orlando 2026, I built this — a full Barbie-pink survival guide with an interactive brand directory, AI route planner, day-of checklist, and community chat. Because beauty is better when we're organized AND glamorous.

My personal obsessions: K-beauty, melanin-friendly skincare, curly haircare, and anything that comes in pink packaging. If it's a Tatcha or Peach & Lily product, I've already bought it twice.

FAQ About This Site

Who built this site? ▼
This site was built by Jem — a beauty enthusiast and expo veteran who attended the inaugural UBW in San Antonio. It's a passion project built to help the community plan smarter.
Is this affiliated with Ulta Beauty? ▼
No — this is an independent fan site created by Jem. All brand info and booth data comes from the official expo map and public sources. This site has no official affiliation with Ulta Beauty, Inc.
How do I use the Route Builder? ▼
Head to the Resources page, click "Route Builder," select your beauty interests and skin focus, adjust the priority sliders, and hit "Generate My Route." The AI will create a personalized 10-booth itinerary with real booth numbers, walk times, and sample values.
Can I download the expo map? ▼
Yes! Scroll up to the Map Download section on this page or click the "Download Map" button. It's the official Ulta Beauty World 2026 expo map sourced from the SpotMe event app.
