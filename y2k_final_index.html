Survival Tips
01
Arrive at 8AM — not 9AM
Doors open 8AM for check-in. Fenty, Cécred, and Rare Beauty booths have 40+ min lines by 9:15AM.
02
Split up with your crew
Divide and conquer. Cover different zones simultaneously, text each other about hidden gem booths and freebies.
03
Hit high-ROI booths first
Celebrity brand booths — Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty — draw the longest lines early.
04
Bring multiple tote bags
No wheeled bags (except $50 Ulta roller). Some booths give away bags you can reuse all day.
05
Use the on-site shipping station
Flying home? Box up your haul at the on-site station. Use our Shipping Calculator to plan costs ahead.
06
Use the Expo Planner
Build your route, track your checklist, calculate ROI, run the Girl Math budget analyzer — all in our free Planner.
