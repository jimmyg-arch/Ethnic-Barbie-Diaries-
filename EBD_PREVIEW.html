Survival Tips
01
Arrive at 8AM — not 9AM
Doors open 8AM for check-in. Fenty, Cécred, and Rare Beauty booths have 40+ min lines by 9:15AM.
02
Split up with your crew
Divide and conquer. Cover different zones simultaneously — text each other about hidden gem booths and freebies.
03
Hit high-ROI booths first
Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty draw the longest lines. Hit those in the first 90 minutes.
04
Bring multiple tote bags
No wheeled bags except the $50 Ulta roller. Some booths give away bags you can reuse all day.
05
Use the on-site shipping station
Flying home? Box up your haul on-site. Use our Shipping Calculator to plan costs ahead.
06
Use the EBD Expo Planner
Build your route, track your checklist, run the Girl Math budget analyzer — all in our free Planner.
