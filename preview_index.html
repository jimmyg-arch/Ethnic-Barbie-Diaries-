Survival Tips
01
Arrive at 8AM — not 9AM
Doors open at 8AM for check-in. The expo starts at 9AM sharp. Fenty, Cécred, and Rare Beauty booths have 40+ minute lines by 9:15AM.
02
Split up with your crew
Divide and conquer. Cover different zones simultaneously, then text each other when you find hidden gem booths or freebies.
03
Hit high-ROI booths first
Celebrity brand booths — Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty — draw the longest lines early. Hit those in the first 90 minutes.
04
Bring multiple tote bags
No wheeled bags allowed (except the $50 Ulta roller). Some booths give away bags you can reuse throughout the day.
05
Use the on-site shipping station
Flying home? Box up your haul at the on-site shipping station. Domestic only — boxes and labels available. Use our shipping cost calculator to plan ahead.
06
Use the Expo Planner tool
Build your custom route, track your checklist, calculate your haul ROI, and run the Girl Math budget analyzer — all in our free Planner.
