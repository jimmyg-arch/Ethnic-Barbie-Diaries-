Pro Tips from Expo Veterans
01
Arrive at 8AM — not 9AM
Doors open at 8AM for check-in. Expo starts 9AM sharp. Fenty, Cécred, and Rare Beauty booths have 40+ minute lines by 9:15AM.
02
Hit celebrity booths in the first 90 minutes
Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty — long lines form early. These are your highest ROI stops. Get there first.
03
Use our Shipping Calculator to plan your haul
Compare USPS, UPS, FedEx rates before the event. Know your shipping budget so you're not surprised at the on-site shipping station.
