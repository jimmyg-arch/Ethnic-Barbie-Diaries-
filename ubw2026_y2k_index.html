SURVIVAL TIPS
1
Arrive at 8AM — NOT 9AM
Doors open at 8AM for check-in. Expo starts 9AM sharp. The Fenty, Cécred, and Rare Beauty booths have 40+ min lines by 9:15AM.
2
Split up with your crew
Divide and conquer. Cover different zones simultaneously, then text each other when you find hidden gem booths or freebies.
3
Hit high-ROI booths first
Celebrity brand booths — Cécred, Fenty, PATTERN, Pat McGrath, Rare Beauty — draw the longest lines early. Hit those in the first 90 minutes.
4
Bring MULTIPLE tote bags
No wheels allowed (except $50 Ulta roller). Pack multiple large tote bags. Some booths give away bags you can reuse all day.
5
Use the shipping station
Flying home? Box up your haul at the on-site shipping station before you leave. Domestic only — boxes and labels available.
6
Use our Expo Planner
Build your custom route, track your checklist, and log visited booths in real-time on your phone. Zero wasted time, maximum haul.
