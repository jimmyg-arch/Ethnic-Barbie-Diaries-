April 16, 2026

Full Day Schedule

Night before — April 15
📋 Final prep night
Lay out your outfit, charge all devices, pack your bags, print or screenshot your ticket, check your route plan one more time.
6:30 – 7:00 AM
⏰ Wake up + get ready
Give yourself at least 90 min to get ready, eat breakfast, and load the car. Florida traffic on I-Drive can be unpredictable.
7:30 AM
🚗 Depart for OCCC
Head to 9800 International Drive, Orlando, FL 32819. Allow 20–30 min for parking + walking to entrance. Parking is $20 cashless — West Parking Lot.
📍 9800 International Drive, Orlando FL
8:00 AM sharp
🎟️ CHECK-IN OPENS
Lobby doors open at 8AM for registration. Have your government-issued ID + digital ticket ready. Grab your wristband and swag bag claim ticket.
📍 Main entrance lobby — OCCC West Building
9:00 AM sharp
🎉 EXPO FLOOR OPENS
Doors open to the full expo floor. Head RIGHT toward Zone C/D first — Fenty (2009), Rare Beauty (2003), Cécred (1903), Pat McGrath (1821) before lines form.
📍 Expo floor — all 200+ booths open
9:00 – 10:30 AM
🔥 Priority booth window
Hit all high-traffic celebrity brand booths before the queues reach 30+ min. Use your pre-built route. This is your golden window.
📍 Zone C & D — booths 1800–2500
11:00 AM – 12:00 PM
💄 Zone B sweep
Work through Zone B (booths 1000–1749) — PATTERN, Tatcha, tarte, NARS, Charlotte Tilbury, Drunk Elephant, JVN, COSRX.
📍 Zone B — booths 1000–1749
12:00 – 1:00 PM
🍕 Lunch + drop bags at storage
Grab food from on-site vendors, drop heavy bags at the free luggage storage area. Stay hydrated — convention AC is deceptively drying.
📍 Food vendors + luggage storage area
1:00 – 3:00 PM
🌸 Zone A sweep
Crowds thin after lunch — perfect time for Zone A (booths 100–999): CeraVe, Lancôme, Kylie, NYX, Charlotte Tilbury, Kiehl's, Sol de Janeiro.
📍 Zone A — booths 100–999
3:00 PM
📬 Ship your haul home
If you're flying, box up your haul at the on-site shipping station now — before the end-of-day rush. Domestic shipping only.
📍 On-site shipping station — near registration
4:00 PM
🎁 SWAG BAG PICKUP
You MUST be present to receive your $2,000+ value swag bag. Use your claim ticket from check-in. Don't leave without it!
📍 Swag bag pickup area — check event signage
5:00 PM
🚪 Expo closes
Expo floor closes at 5PM. Luggage storage closes at 5:30PM. Make sure you've collected everything before heading out!
📍 All exits — OCCC West Building
